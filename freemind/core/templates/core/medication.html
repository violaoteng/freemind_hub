Medications

Learn about medications that can support your mental well-being.

Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitors (SSRIs)

Examples: Prozac, Zoloft, Lexapro, Paxil, Celexa.

Uses: Depression, anxiety, OCD, PTSD, panic disorder.

Side Effects: Nausea, weight changes, insomnia, sexual dysfunction.

Serotonin-Norepinephrine Reuptake Inhibitors (SNRIs)

Examples: Cymbalta, Effexor XR, Pristiq, Fetzima.

Uses: Depression, anxiety, chronic pain, fibromyalgia.

Side Effects: High blood pressure, dizziness, fatigue.

ADHD Medications

Stimulants: Ritalin, Adderall, Vyvanse.

Non-Stimulants: Strattera, Intuniv, Kapvay.

Uses: ADHD, focus improvement.

Side Effects: Insomnia, appetite changes, heart rate increase.
